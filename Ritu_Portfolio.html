About Me

Where Field Research Meets Real-World Impact

I'm an agrologist whose work lives at the intersection of applied research, variety development, and producer engagement. My roots are in potato farming — a family business spanning 25 years — and my career has taken that foundation into multi-location trials, precision agriculture technology, and agricultural innovation partnerships across Canada.

At The Little Potato Company, I led Eastern Canada's potato variety trials end-to-end, using Genstat and Excel to translate field data into variety advancement decisions. At DNA Harvest Partners, I built Canadian partnerships for AI-driven crop improvement technology. And I've been working directly with growers — on the ground, in the field — for over seven years.

I hold a Master's in Environmental Science (University of Guelph), a postgrad in Agricultural Business, and a B.Sc. Agriculture. I'm open to permanent, contract, or fixed-term roles anywhere in Canada — and equally glad to volunteer my time on projects related to sustainability and environmental stewardship.
